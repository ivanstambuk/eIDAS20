                                                                      A Mobile Driving Licence (mDL) is an electronic attestation of attributes that proves a person’s right to drive
                                                                      a particular kind of vehicle on public roads. Following the possible adoption of the new Driving Licence
                                                                      Directive, transport authorities would be required to issue driving licences as mDLs by default from second
                                                                      half of 2029 (tbc). This does not preclude the issuance and recognition of mobile driving licences by Member
                                                                      States for national usage already earlier.

                                                                      There are various scenarios in which a person may need to prove their right to drive a vehicle.

                                                                      Roadside checks by the traffic police
                                                                      At a roadside check the police can check the driving licence, along with other standard documentation,
                                                                      including insurance, roadworthiness, possible emission zone permit, etc.
