                                                                      The European Parking Card for persons with disabilities (EPC) will promote the free movement for
                                                                      persons with disabilities and facilitate their short stays in Member States other than the one in which they
                                                                      reside. The EPC is issued as a proof of the right to use parking conditions and facilities, reserved for persons
                                                                      with disabilities. Each Member State defines its own application procedure and eligibility criteria.

                                                                      The EPC is established by Directive (EU) 2024/2841 together with the European Disability Card (EDC)
                                                                      (see separate manual). The EPC will be mutually recognised in all Member States and will replace
                                                                      all existing parking cards at national, regional or local level issued in accordance with Recommendation
                                                                      98/376/EC by 5 December 2029. The issuance of the physical EPC displayed on the vehicle dashboard
                                                                      is mandatory. Member States have the option to complement it with a digital version. The Member
                                                                      States have the option to link a EPC to a specific licence plate number. The directive has EEA relevance.
                                                                      Its beneficiaries are the Union citizens and their family members. The complementary Directive (EU)
                                                                      2024/2842 extends the provisions of Directive (EU) 2024/2841 and both cards to third-country nationals

Use case implementation
Existing implementations
Directive (EU) 2024/2841 requires Member States to issue the physical EPC and enables, but does not
require, a digital version of the card.


Legislation
The EPC is established by Directive (EU) 2024/2841 (same as for the European Disability Card), which
is currently in force. More specifically, Directive (EU) 2024/2841 defines the physical EPC and allows
(but does not require) a digital version. Member States shall adopt transposition laws by 5 June 2027 and
will apply them as from 5 June 2028.
