                                                                      A Digital Travel Credential (DTC) is a digital representation of the user’s travel document such as a passport,
                                                                      identity card, or another travel document. Travel documents with chips are already composed of both a
                                                                      virtual and a physical component. A DTC (i.e. the virtual component as specified by ICAO) contains essentially
                                                                      the same information as that in the chip of a travel document*.

                                                                      A EUDI Wallet user can store a DTC in their EUDI Wallet and use it for the submission of the travel document
                                                                      information to border authorities prior to travel, facilitating pre-screening and thereby shortening the checks
                                                                      performed at the border. In addition to the DTC, the user may also need to submit other travel data, such as
                                                                      the date of travel, flight number, etc. The reception of the DTC and other travel data is handled by a Traveller
                                                                      Router, which collects and verifies the information and forwards it to the relevant border authorities.

                                                                      The use case applies to extra-EU travel as well as to travel to the Schengen area from Ireland and Cyprus
                                                                      and back. It applies to both inbound and outbound travel. Regarding the outbound travel to third countries,
