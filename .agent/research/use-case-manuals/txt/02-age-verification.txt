                                                                      The Age Verification use case allows citizens to prove they are above a specific age threshold (e.g., over
                                                                      16, 18, or 21) using a verifiable digital credential stored in their EUDI Wallet. The wallet enables confirming
                                                                      age without revealing other personal details such as the full birthdate or name.

                                                                      Age Verification is performed according to a very strict privacy approach, by presenting an attestation
                                                                      that contains a boolean age claim (e.g., age_over_18 = true). The claim can be included in a dedicated
                                                                      attestation or be part of a broader set of attributes in another attestation, such as a mobile driving licence
                                                                      or a photo ID. The use of selected disclosure allows the verifier service to perform an age check without
                                                                      learning the date of birth or other personal details.

                                                                      The use case is applicable to both online and proximity scenarios. Examples of scenarios where age
                                                                      verification may be required include:

                                                                                                                Value for public authorities
                                                                                                                Implementing age verification through the EUDI Wallet allows public authorities to deliver on their
                                                                                                                digitalisation and privacy-by-design commitments to citizens. By embedding privacy-preserving
                                                                                                                age checks into national wallet systems, authorities can promote safer online environments while giving
                                                                                                                citizens control over their personal data.

                                                                                                                Verifiable age proofs help authorities ensure compliance with age-related regulations, reducing
                                                                                                                reliance on less secure self-declaration methods. By providing regulated industries with a consistent
                                                                                                                verification mechanism, authorities also improve enforcement efficiency while minimising the risk of data
                                                                                                                breaches and misuse.


                                                                                                                Economic value and potential market size
                                                                                                                Issuers: The cost impact is low relative to other wallet functions since age proofs can be derived from other
                                                                                                                wallet-based identity data such as PID. The value lies in improved compliance services that governments
                                                                                                                can offer to regulated industries (e.g., gambling, adult content, alcohol sales) without privacy compromise.

                                                                                                                Relying Parties: As the solution is based on harmonised EU-level specifications, relying parties avoid
                                                                                                                the need to implement support for different national solutions in their services, decreasing costs.

Legislation
Article 28 of the Digital Services Act (DSA) requires providers to put in place appropriate and
proportionate measures to ensure a high level of privacy, safety, and security of minors, on their service.
Guidelines adopted pursuant to Article 28 further specify cases in which age verification should be used.
Examples of these include the sale of alcohol or tobacco, access to pornographic or gambling content, or
cases in which a minimum age is prescribed by law.

Data model, format and proof mechanisms
Detailed technical specifications of the EU Age Verification solution provide complete information
and examples of the attestation and proof mechanisms.

The data model for the proof of age attestation is based on the attribute schema specified in
ISO/IEC 18013-5 (Mobile Driving Licence Application) and ISO/IEC 23220-2 (Data objects and encoding
rules for generic eID systems).

The attestation format used is ISO mDoc.

Issuance
Proof of age attestations may be issued by entities placed on the trusted list of proof of age attestation
providers deployed and managed by the Commission.

OpenID4VCI (OpenID for Verifiable Credential Issuance) may be used for delivering the proof of age
attestation to the wallet, however issuers may choose to rely on alternative protocols. Implementation that
relies on OpenID4VCI is included in available open-source implementation of the EU age verification solution.
