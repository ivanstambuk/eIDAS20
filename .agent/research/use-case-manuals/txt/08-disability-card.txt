                                                                      The European Disability Card (EDC) will promote the free movement for persons with disabilities and
                                                                      facilitate their short stays in Member States other than the one in which they reside. The EDC will serve as
                                                                      a proof of recognised disability status or of an entitlement to specific services based on a disability.
                                                                      The card will grant equal access to special conditions or preferential treatment for a wide range of services,
                                                                      activities or facilities, offered to persons with disabilities in the visited Member State. Special conditions
                                                                      or preferential treatment could take the form of discounted museum tickets, free access, priority access,
                                                                      personal assistance, etc.

                                                                      The EDC is established by Directive (EU) 2024/284, together with the European Parking Card for persons with
                                                                      disabilities. The Directive provides for EU‑wide recognition of the EDC and foresees both physical and digital
                                                                      versions of the card, with the latter intended to be usable “by means of a digital identity wallet at Union
                                                                      level.” It will be possible for persons with disabilities to request the physical version of the card, the digital
                                                                      version or both. For the physical EDC, Member States are obliged to introduce it within a reasonable time

                                                                      1. The user opens a transport booking website to buy discounted train tickets for an upcoming trip
                                                                         to another Member State.

                                                                      2. During checkout, the user detects that a disability discount is available and applies for it. Then the

                                                                      3. The user selects the EDC stored in their EUDI Wallet. With a single tap, the EUDI Wallet creates

                                                                         the disability. The site applies the discounted fare and finalises the ticket purchase. The user receives
                                                                         a confirmation email without any uploads, scans, or physical documents required.

Legislation
The EDC is mandatory for all Member States and established by Directive (EU) 2024/2841 (same as for the
European Parking Card for persons with disabilities). Member States shall adopt transposition laws by 5 June
2027, and these will apply from 5 June 2028.

Current formats for the national disability cards and upcoming formats for the EDC:

•   National documents:
    National disability cards and disability certificates can be issued by Member States according to national
    law; however, they are out of scope of the Directive and may be not accepted in other Member States.

•   Physical EDC with digital features:
    Annex I to the Directive defines the format for the physical EDC with a digital feature (QR code on the
    reverse side). Similar to the approach followed for the EU Digital COVID Certificates, when scanned, this
    QR code would authenticate the document contents, including a check of its possible revocation. The QR
    code and, where appropriate, other digital features on the physical card are to be defined in a delegated
    act envisaged to be adopted by the Commission by 5 December 2025.

•   Digital EDC - credential:
    the physical EDC shall be complemented by an accessible digital version by the Member States within
    a reasonable time after the Commission has laid down the corresponding technical specifications through
    implementing acts. There is no deadline for the adoption of these implementing acts. The specifications
    are envisaged to build on the experience on digitalisation of certificates and documents, such as the EU
    Digital COVID Certificate and the empowering provision refers to enabling the use of the EDC in “a digital
    identity wallet at Union level”.

Member States shall designate one or more competent authorities or bodies responsible for the issuance,
renewal and withdrawal of the European Disability Card and the European Parking Card for persons with
disabilities..
